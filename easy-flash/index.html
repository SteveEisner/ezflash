Inspect a connected controller’s serial report without flashing, erasing, resetting, or opening an install path. Opening USB serial can reboot some controllers; nothing happens until you choose Inspect.

No controller inspected

Choose Inspect to request a controller and capture a bounded diagnostic banner.

What the evidence means

Only an exact reported model identifies Dig2Go, Athom C3, or Waveshare S3. A generic ESP32, C3, or S3 chip report alone leaves the controller Unknown.

AVAILABLE, INACTIVE, and healthy button reports are healthy states. STUCK_BUTTON is a problem. Missing telemetry proves neither health nor failure.
