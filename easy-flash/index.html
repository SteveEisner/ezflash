Recovery guidance

If the banner says rescue or safe mode, LEDs and network are intentionally absent. The device may still be recoverable. Use Flash only after confirming the exact target; Diagnose never starts recovery on its own.

Missing or unsupported telemetry is not a healthy-state signal.
